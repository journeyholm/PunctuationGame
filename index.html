How to Play

A mischievous gremlin named Grammarlin has stolen the 12 Punctuation Marks and thrown the realms into chaos. Win them back, mark by mark!

  • Move: Arrow Keys or WASD (hold to keep walking). You can also click or tap any tile to walk there.
  • Talk: walk up to a glowing guardian and press E, Space, or the TALK button. Click a guardian to walk over and talk.
  • Trials: each guardian asks 3 questions. Get all three right in one try to restore the mark. Answer with the mouse or keys 123. Missed one? Try again with new questions.
  • Study first: choose “Study the Mark” to read the rules before a trial. Everything you learn is saved in the Codex.
  • Collect 9 hidden Golden Quills for big bonus points.
  • Travel: restore the Question Mark to power the Tower Elevator, then visit the Sky Tower and the Depths of Grammar.
  • Win: restore all 12 marks, open the Door of Expression, and defeat Grammarlin!

Scoring: quick, correct answers and answer streaks earn bonus points. Your ⌛ time bonus starts at 3,600 and drains 2 points per second — whatever is left is added at the end. Restore every mark without a mistake for the Perfect Run bonus! Use 🔊 to hear a question read aloud.

Teachers: click 📊 Progress (or press P) anytime to see how a student is doing: marks restored, accuracy, tries, hints, and missed questions. The clock pauses while it’s open, and you can print the report.
