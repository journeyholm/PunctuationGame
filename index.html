How to Play

A mischievous gremlin named Grammarlin has scattered the 12 Punctuation Marks and thrown the world into chaos. Restore order, mark by mark.

  • Explore three realms: the Ground, the Sky Tower, and the Depths.
  • Move with the Arrow Keys or W A S D. Press E (or the TALK button) to talk and interact.
  • Talk to each guardian and pass their 3-question trial — all three correct in one go to win the mark.
  • Collect 9 hidden Golden Quills for a big score bonus.
  • Win all 12 marks, then face Grammarlin in the Chaos Showdown.
